Gwin's Readers Club

Privacy & Child Safety Policy

Last updated 2026. Written in plain language because many of our members are children.

Our commitment

Gwin's Readers Club is built for children and young people, so we collect as little personal information as possible, we ask a parent or guardian's permission for members under 18, and we give parents/guardians a way to review or remove their child's data at any time by contacting us.

What we collect
  • Account basics: full name, email, and a password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text).
  • Optional profile details you choose to share: school, state, reading interests, a short bio.
  • For members under 18: an age range and a parent/guardian's name and email, used only for consent and safeguarding.
  • Reading activity within the platform: progress, journal entries (private to you), reviews, and community posts.
  • Staff profile photos, where a staff member chooses to upload one — members never upload a photo of themselves, only an illustrated avatar.
What we don't do
  • We never ask children to upload a real photo of themselves.
  • We don't sell or share personal data with advertisers.
  • We don't solicit donations from student members — see our Support page for details.
  • We don't process payment card data anywhere in the app.
Parents & guardians

If your child has an account, you can ask us to show you what information we hold, correct it, or delete the account entirely. Reach us through the Safety Center report form, and mention you're a parent/guardian request — we'll follow up using the contact details you provide.

Data protection

We aim to align with the principles of Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPR/NDPA): lawfulness, data minimization, purpose limitation, and accountability. Passwords are hashed, sessions expire automatically, and access to member data is limited to staff who need it to run the platform.

Questions or concerns

Use the Safety Center report form for anything urgent, or reach out through our social channels linked in the footer.
