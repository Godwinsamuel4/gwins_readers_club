Keeping Gwin's Readers Club a safe place to grow

This platform is built for children and young readers. Here's how we protect members, and how to reach us if something feels wrong.

What we do to keep members safe
  • Every account under 18 requires a parent or guardian's name, email, and explicit consent before it's created.
  • Staff (admins, moderators, mentors) are verified adults; members choose an illustrated avatar rather than uploading a real photo of themselves.
  • All community posts, reviews, and messages can be reported and are reviewed by our moderation team.
  • Sessions automatically sign out after 20 minutes of inactivity, protecting accounts on shared or school devices.
  • We collect the minimum information needed to run the platform — see our Privacy & Child Safety Policy.
In an emergency

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services right away.

In Nigeria: NAPTIP (human trafficking & abuse) toll-free line 0800-CALL-NAPTIP (0800-2255-62784), or the Nigeria Police Force emergency number 112.

Report a concern

Use this form for anything that worries you — inappropriate behavior from another member or a mentor, a concerning message, or anything else. You don't need to be logged in to use it, and reports go straight to our moderation team.
